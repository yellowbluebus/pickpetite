Frequently Asked Questions

What is PickPetite?

PickPetite is an innovative app that blends European design with Japanese minimalism to offer personalized home improvement solutions. It focuses on creating a haven of minimalism, health, and harmony in living spaces.

What makes PickPetite different?

PickPetite personalizes recommendations unlike any other home improvement app. We cater to various needs—whether it's designing kitchens for healthier eating, bedrooms for better sleep, or living rooms that inspire movement.

Who designs for PickPetite?

Our app features recommendations by award-winning designers, ensuring quality and good taste in every suggestion. We combine human intelligence with technology for the best results.

Why choose PickPetite?

The app stands out for its ability to declutter your space and nurture a healthier state of mind and body. It's ideal for those seeking a more nourishing, vital, and wholesome living environment with ecological awareness.

How does it work?

Simply upload photos of your space, and PickPetite's intelligence will guide you in making smart, conscious choices for your home. We offer small, healthy, and joyful solutions that adapt to your life.

Is it expensive?

Good taste doesn't have to be expensive! PickPetite helps you make affordable, lasting, joy-filled choices. We eliminate choice anxiety and help you find the best value for your budget.
